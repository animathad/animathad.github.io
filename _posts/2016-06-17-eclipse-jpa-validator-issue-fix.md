If your eclipse JPA Validator kicks in every time there is a modification to any file. You can disable in the Project Facets.

![](/assets/img/posts/eclipse-jpa-validator-issue-fix/0030c-11wcfe241rdhag4lhyji2-w.png)

Just uncheck the JPA option in facets and you should be able to get rid of the issue.
