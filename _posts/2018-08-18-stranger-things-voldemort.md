Shameless copy of Harry Potter plot!

So here I was all excited for Season 2 to kick off. My popcorn is ready, I am curled up with my blanket on my sofa & what do I get ? A shameless copy of the Potter plot.

### **The connection between Will & the Evil**

So this kid Will is being spied on by the strange thing. Whatever he sees he can see & vice versa. Hmm! Where have I read this before ? Mr. Harry Potter can you please guide Will how you disconnected from the Voldemort subscription ?

Again how original! The only thing remaining was Will speaking “stranger” tongue. May be it is for the next season?
