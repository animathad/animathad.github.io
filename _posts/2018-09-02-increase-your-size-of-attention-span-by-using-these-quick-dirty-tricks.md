Make a boring topic interesting by using these 5 tricks.

Often I am easily bored and tend to have an attention span of a *Insert analogy here*. But you get it, I am too bored to even come up with an analogy.

![](/assets/img/posts/increase-your-size-of-attention-span-by-using-these-quick-dirty-tricks/1-dQ0EMIizoK3VdQEdb75MMw.jpeg)

So I compared things that I am interested in and tried to imitate the same to boring topics. I am often live and breathe topics which are interesting. But not so much for others.

> “I am only passionately curious” — Albert Einstein

So Albert Einstein believes he is not smart, he is just passionately curious.

What if I am not passionately curious ? Which I am always ? Often many things in life are “Jobs to be Done!” but we are not passionate about it. Doesn’t mean we cannot be curious.

### How to build curiosity on an alien topic?

![](/assets/img/posts/increase-your-size-of-attention-span-by-using-these-quick-dirty-tricks/1-yh1nUxh-pUI8z_z5MEgsRw.jpeg)

Ask 5 random(at least) questions about the topic. If I am reading about a complex technical heavy engineering nerd friendly topic, I would ask

1. At a high level (in layman’s terms) what does this do ?
2. What is the impact on every day life ?
3. What were we doing before this ?
4. Why now ?
5. Why is so important ?

Always go with at-least 5 questions. It can be random doesn’t have to be the ones above. Reading a boring material is easier if you are looking for something. It better be small. Once you are done getting answers to these take a break. You are free! Come back later. But always ensure to comeback. **Problem with short attention span is not that you don’t focus for long, It is that you never comeback to the same topic.** If you always comeback to the topic even a week late, reading the same thing twice gives you a lot of gotchas.

Pro level:

If you don’t understand something don’t break your head move to the next paragraph or page. Try to make a small summary of those pieces. After a couple of paragraphs you things will try to make sense.
