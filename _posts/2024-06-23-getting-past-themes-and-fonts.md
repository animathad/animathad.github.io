I am one of those guys who gets obsessed about the cover of the book rather than the content.

I will spend hours and hours on getting the right theme. I will change fonts so many times. I will keep the “meta” iterations going that I get exhausted.

I don’t focus on writing actual content. I like to distract myself with the look and feels.

Maybe it’s procrastination. My escape instead of actually writing things down.
