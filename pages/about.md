<p class="about-lead">I'm an engineering leader at Adobe, working at the intersection of developer platforms and AI — building the systems that let thousands of engineers ship faster.</p>

I manage an engineering team in Adobe's **Developer Platforms** organization in San Jose. Day to day, that means the internal platforms, pipelines, and tooling that Adobe's engineers build on — and, most recently, a new charter around **AI factories**: the infrastructure and workflows that put AI to work for our roughly 15,000 developers.

I'm drawn to the unglamorous layer of software — the platforms, build systems, and release pipelines that rarely get talked about but decide how quickly everyone else can move. I care about developer experience, the leverage good abstractions create, and what happens when you point modern AI at those problems.

Before Adobe I worked at **Rakuten Intelligence** (formerly Slice Technologies). I hold an **M.S. in Computer Engineering from Arizona State University**. In 2022 I spoke at **ArgoCon** about running Adobe's release pipelines on Argo Workflows.

This site is where I write it all down — finished essays in the [blog](/posts), and rougher, evolving notes in the [garden](/notes). Opinions here are my own.
